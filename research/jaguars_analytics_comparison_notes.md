Source: https://www.jaguars.com/team/front-office-roster/

Source: https://operations.nfl.com/game-operations-logistics/technology/performance-tracking-data-next-gen-stats
- MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference’s profile notes that the Jaguars created a Football Technology and Analytics group under Tony Khan in 2012, covering coaching and scouting support plus technology applications in player development, injury prevention, game analysis, and player evaluation. This is historic background, not a disclosure of the Jaguars’ current product stack. Source: https://www.sloansportsconference.com/people/tony-khan

## Guardrails for comparison

- The Jaguars do not publicly disclose their complete data feeds, models, dashboards, scouting workflows, proprietary evaluations, or system accuracy. Do not claim feature parity or infer undocumented capabilities as facts.
- TacticalEdge should be framed as a **coach-facing product for fast film-to-action workflow**, not an NFL front-office replacement.
- Areas that can be compared directly: usability, time-to-insight, automated film outputs, visualization, preparedness tooling, and accessibility for high-school/college staffs.
- Areas that should be acknowledged as likely NFL advantages but not presented as verified architecture: proprietary multi-year datasets, dedicated quantitative staff, private player tracking/injury/medical information, and full organizational integration.

## Comparison implications

- The correct comparison is not a single public "Tony Khan app." It is TacticalEdge AI versus a confidential, staff-operated **NFL analytics ecosystem** led in part by Tony Khan, with a dedicated analytics, decision-science, systems, software, and data-science team.
- Next Gen Stats tracking gives NFL clubs raw precision TacticalEdge cannot recreate from ordinary end-zone or sideline film alone. TacticalEdge’s current player paths, circles, labels, and timestamps should therefore remain clearly described as AI-estimated / film-derived unless the platform begins ingesting verified tracking data.
- TacticalEdge’s strongest differentiated position is turning complicated film review into a fast, visual, coach-ready workflow for teams that do not have an NFL analytics department. It should not claim equivalent NFL data fidelity or proprietary model depth.

## Jaguars partnership hypothesis

TacticalEdge is not immediately a replacement for Jaguars analytics, decision science, film, scouting, performance, or data-platform work. Its credible role is a **coach-facing intelligence experience layer**: it can turn validated internal data and video evidence into fast, visual, explainable teaching, meeting, and game-preparation workflows.

The partnership case is strongest when TacticalEdge is offered as a controlled, non-production pilot for one narrowly defined workflow, such as translating approved opponent-tendency outputs into evidence-linked teaching clips and practice emphasis. This avoids any implication that the product should replace the Jaguars’ existing analytics staff or gain broad access to sensitive player, medical, scouting, or proprietary football data.

### Current readiness assessment

TacticalEdge is product-differentiated in visual coaching workflows: report-to-clip navigation, interactive diagrams, 3D play visualization, War Room workspaces, call sheets, and broad LLM-assisted analysis panels. It is not yet NFL-enterprise ready because its advanced analytics routers generate structured estimates from highlights contained in the pre-existing scouting report rather than measuring every play directly from calibrated tracking data. It needs evidence links, provenance, human-review controls, formal evaluation, and data-governance architecture before any serious NFL data pilot.
