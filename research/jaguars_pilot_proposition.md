# TacticalEdge AI — Jaguars Pilot Proposition

## Positioning

**The Finnese Group LLC** proposes TacticalEdge AI as a coach-facing, evidence-linked intelligence layer that converts approved film and data into visual teaching packages, opponent-preparation summaries, and staff-ready action plans. The product is not presented as a replacement for Jaguars Football Analytics and Strategy, decision science, film systems, or existing data infrastructure.

## The one-sentence pitch

> "Your analytics group already produces intelligence. TacticalEdge is the visual delivery layer that helps coaches move from approved data and film to an evidence-backed teaching package faster."

## Narrow pilot concept

Run a four-week offline historical-film pilot using three to five approved opponent games. The Jaguars team chooses one workflow: for example, an opponent-tendency teaching package for a weekly position meeting. TacticalEdge ingests only approved historical inputs, produces evidence-linked clips, draft diagrams, and practice-emphasis recommendations, and requires a staff reviewer to approve or reject every substantive output.

## Pilot deliverables

| Deliverable | What TacticalEdge provides | Jaguars staff role |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence-linked tendency package | Coach-readable summary with every claim connected to source clips/play IDs | Validate usefulness and accuracy |
| Teaching-ready clip sequence | Timed clips, overlays, and coaching notes for a position or unit meeting | Confirm clips and messaging |
| Visual concept package | Editable route/gap/coverage visualizations using team-approved vocabulary | Review football language and corrections |
| Evaluation report | Time saved, accuracy score, reviewer approval rate, and workflow feedback | Set benchmarks and decide whether to extend |

## What not to pitch

Do not pitch "we built a better analytics department." Do not request unrestricted tracking, medical, roster, salary, or confidential scouting data. Do not promise exact performance predictions, injury outcomes, or NFL-grade tracking precision from ordinary video. Those claims create both credibility and governance problems.

## Primary conversation targets

The credible first audience is the Football Analytics and Strategy group, especially the people closest to systems, quantitative research, and coach-facing implementation. A warm introduction to football operations or an innovation/product stakeholder is stronger than a cold request to ownership. The ask should be a fifteen-minute product discovery conversation, not immediate access to sensitive data.

## Ask for the first meeting

"I built a visual AI layer for turning film and approved football data into evidence-linked coaching packages. I am not asking to replace your analytics stack or for live data access. I would value fifteen minutes to understand whether there is one historical, low-risk workflow where a controlled pilot could save staff time while keeping every AI conclusion reviewable."
