## Slide 2: Football coaches spend 15+ hours per week on manual film breakdown

**Key Points:**
- High school and small college coaching staffs spend 15-20 hours per week manually reviewing game film, pausing frame-by-frame, and hand-drawing plays on whiteboards
- Only 3% of high school programs can afford a dedicated film analyst or coordinator — the rest rely on volunteer coaches doing it after their day jobs
- Existing tools like Hudl ($800-2,000+/year) provide video hosting but zero automated analysis — coaches still do all the thinking manually
- The result: coaches burn out, preparation quality drops, and student-athletes miss development opportunities because staff simply cannot keep up
